Software — GamersRival internal admin settings, for staff managing payment gateways and platform rules.
Direction: retain the account theme, Inter typography and brand blue; white or dark account surfaces, consistent six-pixel control radii. The existing brand takes precedence over a new palette deal.

The primary action is saving the current settings form. Existing business actions, encrypted credential storage, permissions and reset restrictions remain the source of truth.

- Workspace: General (timezone and currency), Languages, Sign-in security.
- Finance: Payment gateways, Fees & commission, Referral rewards.
- Operations: Tournament timing, Dispute alerts, and local-only test data tools.
- Desktop: nested section navigation beside a content panel. Compact screens: a grouped section selector. Selecting a panel retains unsaved form inputs, supports direct links, and follows browser back/forward.
- Payments: deposit provider choices, withdrawal provider choices, relevant credentials, certificate upload and provider-specific webhook URLs. Saved credential status does not imply a verified connection.
- Feedback: disabled submit while saving/uploading; success and validation messages; reveal only newly entered credential text; copy webhook URLs with a manual selection fallback.
- Motion: short hover/press feedback with reduced-motion support. No marketing hero, artificial illustration, animation scene, photography or scroll moment in this working admin form.

Voice: direct operational labels, precise financial effects, one action per form. No assumed facts, new brand, placeholder contacts, photography or artwork slots.

Verification: isolated SQLite feature tests for all settings actions, authorization and admin entry pages; existing admin/wallet and full PHP suites; JavaScript navigation/history tests; production build; skill browser screenshots and gate. Real provider calls and live money movement are excluded.
