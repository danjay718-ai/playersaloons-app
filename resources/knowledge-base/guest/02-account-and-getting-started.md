# Account Registration & Sign-In (Guest Guide)

## Creating an Account
- Click **Join Now** or **Create Account** on the homepage.
- You can register using your **email and password** or click **Continue with Google** for faster sign-up.
- When registering with Google for the first time, you will complete a quick onboarding step to choose your username, confirm your date of birth, select your country, and accept the platform Terms & Privacy Policy.

## Email Verification & Security
- Verify your email address after registering to unlock full participation features.
- Players can enable **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)** from their account settings for extra security.
- If you forget your password, use the **Forgot Password?** link on the Sign In page to receive a password reset link by email.

## Eligibility & Policies
- Players must meet the minimum age requirement and reside in a supported region (some countries may be restricted for compliance reasons).
- Review the official **Terms of Service**, **Privacy Policy**, **Cookie Policy**, and competition rules anytime under the **Policies** link in the footer.
- Need help before signing up? Visit the **Contact** page from the navigation menu or footer to send an inquiry to our support team.
