# GamersRival Platform Overview (Guest Guide)

## What is GamersRival?
GamersRival is an online competitive gaming platform where players compete in organized tournaments and 1v1 Head-to-Head (H2H) matches for cash prizes and leaderboard rankings.

## Supported Game Categories
- **Video Games:** Competitive esports and mobile/PC/console titles (such as Valorant, Call of Duty: Mobile, Mobile Legends, and other active catalog titles).
- **Card & Board Games:** Competitive strategy games including **Chess**, **International Draughts**, **Backgammon**, and **Othello (Flipello)** powered by live PlayStrategy match boards.

## Ways to Compete
- **Tournaments:** Scheduled daily, weekly, monthly, and one-time bracket events (free or paid entry) with advertised prize pools.
- **Head-to-Head (1v1):** Direct one-on-one competitive matches between two players.
- **Live Streams & Community:** Watch public tournament streams, read official news and blog updates, and check platform announcements via the "What's New" popup modal on the homepage.

## How It Works (4 Simple Steps)
1. **Create Account:** Register with email or sign in quickly using Google.
2. **Find & Join a Match:** Browse by game category or use the **Find Tournament** quick-finder to join an open tournament.
3. **Play & Submit Results:** Play your match (automatically synced on PlayStrategy board games, or report results directly in the Match Room for video games).
4. **Win & Cash Out:** Prize winnings are credited directly to your GamersRival wallet and can be withdrawn once your account is verified.
