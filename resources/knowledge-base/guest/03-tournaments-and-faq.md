# Finding Tournaments & General FAQ (Guest Guide)

## How to Browse Available Competitions
- **Homepage Game Catalog:** Filter games by **All Games**, **Video Games**, or **Card & Board Games**, or use the search box to find your favorite title.
- **Find Tournament Quick-Finder:** Click **Find Tournament** in the top navigation bar or hero section to filter open competitions by game, category, platform, entry fee (including Free events), and time window.
- **Game Hub Pages:** Open any game to read its overview, browse active upcoming/ongoing/past tournaments, or watch live streams.

## Entry Fees & Prizes
- Some tournaments are **Free to Enter** (sponsored prizes or practice events), while others have a clearly listed entry fee.
- Entry fees and prize pools are displayed in the platform currency before you join.
- To register for a tournament, deposit funds, or withdraw prizes, you must create and sign in to a GamersRival player account.

## Installing the Web App (PWA)
- GamersRival works on both desktop and mobile browsers and supports installing to your home screen via the **Install App** button for a faster, app-like experience.
