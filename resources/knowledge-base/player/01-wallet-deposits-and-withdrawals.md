# Wallet, Deposits, Withdrawals & KYC (Player Guide)

## Checking Your Balance
- Open **Wallet** from the sidebar (desktop) or tap the **Deposit (`+`)** button in the mobile bottom navigation bar.
- Your dashboard also shows your Available Balance (with an eye icon to hide/show it on mobile).

## Making a Deposit
1. Go to **Wallet** and select **Deposit**.
2. Choose a quick amount or type your desired deposit amount.
3. Complete payment through the active checkout gateway (such as **Stripe** or **PayOp**).
4. Once payment is confirmed, the net credited amount is added to your wallet balance and recorded in your transaction history. Any applicable processing fee is displayed clearly before checkout.

## Requesting a Withdrawal
1. Complete **KYC Identity Verification** in your Profile / KYC section if prompted (required before payouts).
2. Go to **Wallet → Withdraw**, enter the withdrawal amount and required payout details, and submit your request.
3. Withdrawal requests are reviewed by our finance team and processed through the configured payout provider. Any applicable withdrawal fee is shown before confirmation.

## Refunds & Auto-Cancelled Tournaments
- If a tournament does not reach the minimum required participants before starting, it is automatically cancelled and all entry fees are refunded back to your wallet.
- If you cancel your registration before the registration cutoff window, your entry fee is refunded according to the tournament's cancellation rules.
