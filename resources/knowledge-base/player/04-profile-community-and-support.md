# Profile, Chat, Streams & Announcements (Player Guide)

## Profile & Linked Accounts
- Open **Profile** to update your display name, avatar, in-game usernames/handles, connected **Google** account, connected **PlayStrategy** account, password, and **2FA** security settings.
- If your PlayStrategy token expires or is revoked, reconnect it directly from your Profile page.

## Community & Support
- **Global Chat & Match Chat:** Chat with other players in **Chats** or coordinate directly with your opponent inside the Match Room.
- **Streams:** Watch live streams from **Streams** or inside any Game Hub's **Streams** tab.
- **Announcements & What's New:** Important updates, new partnerships, and promo videos appear in the **"What's New"** popup modal on your dashboard and in the **Official Feed (Announcements)** panel. Once dismissed, an announcement won't pop up again.
- **Contact Support:** For billing, KYC, or general issues, open **Support / Contact** from the menu to submit a ticket to our support team.
