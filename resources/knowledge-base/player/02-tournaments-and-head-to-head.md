# Joining Tournaments & Head-to-Head Matches (Player Guide)

## Finding & Joining Tournaments
- Use **Find Tournament** (on your Player Dashboard or top bar) to filter open events by category, game, platform, entry fee, and start time, then click **View & Join**.
- Or open **Tournaments** from the navigation menu, pick a category tab (**All Games**, **Video Games**, **Card & Board Games**), tap a game card to filter, and switch between **Upcoming**, **Ongoing**, and **Past** events.
- Make sure you have saved the required **in-game username / handle** for that game/platform (you can update game handles in your **Profile**).
- If an entry fee applies, ensure your wallet has enough balance before joining.

## Head-to-Head (1v1) Matches
- Switch to **H2H** from the sidebar or mobile bottom nav to browse and join 1v1 competitive matches.
- View your active and past entries anytime under **My Tournaments** (separated into Tournaments and Head-to-Head tabs).

## Progression, Leaderboards & Squads
- Playing and winning matches earns **XP** that increases your **Platform Level**.
- Check your ranking under **Leaderboards**.
- Create or join a team under **Squads** (when team competitions are active), and follow other players to see who is online on your dashboard.
