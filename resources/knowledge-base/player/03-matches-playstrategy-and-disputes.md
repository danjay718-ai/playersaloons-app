# Match Room, PlayStrategy, Result Submissions & Disputes (Player Guide)

## Using the Match Room
- Once your tournament round begins, open your match from **My Tournaments** or the tournament's **Submit Results** tab.
- At the top of the Match Room you will see your matchup (Side A vs Side B), match status, and both players' **Side A ID** and **Side B ID** so you can add each other in-game quickly.

## Playing PlayStrategy Board Games (Chess, Draughts, Backgammon, Othello)
1. First, link your **PlayStrategy account** from your **Profile** page (using your PlayStrategy Personal OAuth2 token with `challenge:write` permission).
2. When a PlayStrategy match starts, GamersRival automatically creates an official match board and displays a **Play Match** link in the Match Room.
3. Play the game on PlayStrategy — results sync automatically!
4. If the game ends in a **Draw**, a rematch board is automatically generated for a new attempt.
5. If a board is aborted or cannot be synced cleanly, the match is escalated to a tournament admin for review.

## Submitting Match Results (Video Games)
- After finishing your match, select **Win** or **Loss** in the **Submit Results** card and submit.
- Always take a **clear, uncropped screenshot of the final scoreboard** showing both usernames/IGNs and the final score in case a verification is needed.
- If both players agree on the outcome, the match completes immediately and the winner advances.

## Result Conflicts & Disputes
- If both players submit conflicting outcomes (for example, both claim Win), a **Result Conflict** is opened automatically.
- Both players are prompted to upload their **screenshot proof** and an optional written explanation.
- While submitted proof is being reviewed, the Match Room displays a neutral **"Reviewing submitted evidence…"** status.
- If no clear winner can be verified from the screenshots, the dispute is **escalated to a Tournament Admin** who reviews the proof and decides the final ruling.
- Submitting deliberately fake or edited evidence can result in a **dishonest-result strike**, wallet penalty, or account ban (3 confirmed strikes result in a permanent ban).

## Reporting a Completed Match or Appealing a Ruling
- After a match is completed, if you believe there was an error in the ruling or want to report an issue, click **Report Match / Provide Feedback** inside the **Submit Results** card to send an appeal/report directly to the admin team.
